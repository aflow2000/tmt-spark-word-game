Overview

Spark Word at a glance

One word per issue, chosen by hand. Activate an issue when the newsletter ships; the previous one archives itself.

Newsletter workflow

1 Create the issue → 2 pick the word from the bank (used words are blocked) → 3 add the hint and the post-game explanation → 4 Preview game → 5 Activate (or schedule) → 6 export personalised links → 7 paste the email module into the newsletter.

Issues

Every issue, one Spark Word

Only one issue is active at a time. Scheduled issues activate on their publication date if you run admin_activate_due_issues() from pg_cron — or press Activate yourself.

Word bank

The curated answer pool

Five-letter industry terms with a definition and a suggested hint. A word marked used was already an issue's answer and can't be picked again without an explicit override.

Subscribers & links

Personalised game links

Each subscriber has an opaque token. The newsletter link /spark-word/014?t=TOKEN recognises them without an account and never exposes their email.

Export mail-merge links

One row per newsletter subscriber with their personal game link. Pick the format your email tool expects — the Staffbase option produces the custom-data CSV Staffbase Email imports (identifier first), so {{gameUrl}} becomes a personalisation tag.

Import subscribers

Paste CSV (with or without a header): email, first_name, last_name, company. Existing emails are updated and keep their token; new ones get a fresh token.

Analytics

How each issue played

Recipients come from the issue record; clicks are game views that arrived from a newsletter link; everything else is computed from games and events.

Email module

Drop this into the newsletter

Table-based, inline-styled, no JavaScript. Merge fields: {{ISSUE_NUMBER}}, {{GAME_URL}}, {{LAST_PLAYERS}}. The game itself runs on the web — the CTA opens each reader's personal link.
